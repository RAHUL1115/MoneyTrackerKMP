# A month that starts late is named after the month it ends in

People can start their reporting month on any day, usually payday. When the start day is 1 to 19, the month is named after the month it starts in (5 March to 4 April is "March"). When it is 20 or later, it is named after the month it ends in (25 February to 24 March is "March"), because most of its days fall in that month. Budgets, carry-over and the start day saved in a Money Manager backup all rely on this rule, so changing it would rename people's past months.
