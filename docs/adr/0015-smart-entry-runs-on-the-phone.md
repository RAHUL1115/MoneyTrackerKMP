# Smart entry runs on the phone, without a large downloaded model

Smart entry turns typed or spoken text ("chai 20 aur samosa 30") into entries using rules for amounts and dates, plus small models bundled with the app and trained for this. A later version may also use the phone's built-in on-device model on phones that have one. It never sends text to a server, and it does not download a model hundreds of megabytes in size. Categories are each person's own, so predictions are mapped onto that person's categories, and their own past entries win over the model.
