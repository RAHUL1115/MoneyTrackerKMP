MoneyTracker is a free, open-source personal-finance app that works entirely offline. It has no ads, no accounts, no analytics and no internet permission: your data stays on your phone.

<b>Quick daily bookkeeping</b>
• Add income, expenses and transfers with a built-in calculator keypad
• Daily, Calendar and Monthly views, notes and search
• Repeating entries, credit-card installments and transfer fees
• Bookmarks for entries you add often, tags and receipt photos
• Voice entry ("spent 250 on lunch") and category suggestions learned from your notes

<b>Accounts and budgets</b>
• Account groups, net worth and per-account currency
• Credit-card cycles: what's due now, outstanding and upcoming
• Monthly budgets per category and sub-category
• Monthly and yearly statistics with drill-down

<b>Smart, but private</b>
• Optional bank-SMS detection that suggests entries and learns your categories, all on the phone
• Optional location tag on new entries
• Passcode and fingerprint lock

<b>Your data, your files</b>
• Daily automatic snapshots and manual backups
• Export and import full backups, CSV and Excel files
• Switching apps? Restore your Money Manager (.mmbak) backup. MoneyTracker is not affiliated with Realbyte.

<b>And more</b>
• Material You colours, light and dark theme
• Home-screen widgets, launcher shortcuts and a quick-add notification
• 79 languages
