MoneyTracker 0.3.2. Bank SMS detection is more reliable: messages missed while the phone held the app back are picked up every 15 minutes, the switch only shows on when the SMS permission is really granted, and "Keep detection running" shows your phone's battery steps. Failed payments are ignored, reversals count as refunds, and ads in bank alerts no longer hide them. New layout: the review list stays in place as you go, and hidden amounts can be peeked at.
