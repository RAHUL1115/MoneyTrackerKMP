MoneyTracker 0.3.5. First Google Play release; the app is the same as 0.3.4. Includes the Web dashboard: use MoneyTracker from a computer on the same Wi-Fi to see transactions, analytics and accounts, add, edit or delete entries with a full keyboard, and export CSV. The page comes from your phone, never the internet. Optional password, stops by itself when idle. Find it in More (classic) or Settings (new layout).
