MoneyTracker 0.4.2. Bank SMS: each detected payment is its own card with Dismiss and Add. Add opens the editor filled in, starting on whatever is missing (category, account or note). Classic shows the newest payment at the top of Trans. Entries now need a category, and detected payments use the categories you picked before. Icons replace text in the Insights, Stats and Accounts headers. New installs can choose the New or Classic layout when they start.
