MoneyTracker 0.4.0. New: Smart entry. In the classic layout, tap the mic and just say it: "1,200 for new shoes on my credit card yesterday". Amount, category, note, account and date appear as you speak, then the Add screen opens filled in for you to check. It uses your own categories, learns from the notes you save, and also understands Hinglish and Hindi. All on your phone, offline. Also: bank SMS detection skips loan offers, bill reminders and card bill payments, and reads more banks.
