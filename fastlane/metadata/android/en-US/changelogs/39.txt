MoneyTracker 0.4.1. Smart add is now on the Home screen: tap the box to type an entry the way you'd say it ("1,200 for new shoes on my credit card") or tap the mic to speak. It works in both layouts, and fills in the amount, category, note, account and date for you to check. New: pick a backup folder (Documents, SD card or a cloud folder) and daily backups are saved there too. Also: in the new layout the keypad closes after the note, and Activity keeps four header icons.
