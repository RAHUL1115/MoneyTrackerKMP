MoneyTracker 0.5.0 beta 1. Under the hood: the app's code is reorganised so it is easier to read, test and improve; it looks and works the same. Tapping a row or button now highlights exactly that row or button. Better TalkBack support: switches, choices and tabs say what they are and whether they are on. Fixes: tapping a month in the classic trend chart could open the wrong month, and the suggested account could lag behind the note.
